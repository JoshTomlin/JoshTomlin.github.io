About

I am a mathematician with interests in differential geometry and topology, particularly 4-manifolds, gauge theory and contact geometry.

I completed my PhD at the University of Adelaide in June 2025 under the supervision of Dr David Baraglia. In my dissertation, I proved a new, completely general connected sum formula for the families Seiberg–Witten invariant. I accomplished this by first deriving a connected sum formula for a cohomotopy refinement called the Bauer–Furuta invariant. My thesis received a Dean’s Commendation for Doctoral Thesis Excellence.

In Semester 2 2025, I was appointed as an Associate Lecturer at the University of Adelaide, where I was the convener of a third-year course in Complex Analysis. During this time, I published work from my thesis, including a paper containing new results on exotic diffeomorphisms and mapping class groups of 4-manifolds with b+ = 2. I have since left academia and am now a Data Scientist at Amplar Health Home Hospital.

I completed my undergraduate degree at the Australian National University in 2019, obtaining a Bachelor of Philosophy with First Class Honours in Mathematics. In my honours project, supervised by Professor Joan Licata, I developed new methods for tight surgery on contact 3-manifolds with open book decompositions.
